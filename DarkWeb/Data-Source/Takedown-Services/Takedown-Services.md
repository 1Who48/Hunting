## Flare Takedown Services

## Overview

Flare's **Takedown Services** streamline the process of requesting takedowns for various threat exposures, such as malicious domains, code repository leaks, social media threats, and more. This significantly reduces the risks associated with threat actors exploiting these vectors in attacks against your business or customers.

## Key Features

1. **Malicious Look-Alike Domains**: Takedowns for domains used in phishing or attack infrastructure.
2. **Paste Sites**: Removal of leaked credentials and personally identifiable information (PII).
3. **Source Code Repositories**: Takedowns for leaked secrets, personal information, defamatory content, and more.
4. **Cloud Infrastructure Buckets**: Removal of leaked secrets, PII, and malicious content from services like AWS S3 and Azure Blob Storage.
5. **Social Media**: Takedowns for impersonation, fraud, scams, and other violations on platforms like Meta properties, LinkedIn, and X.

## How Takedowns Work

### Initiating a Takedown Request

- **Within the Flare Application**: Use the request takedown option from a related look-alike domain, source code, paste site, or cloud bucket Flare event card within the app. After clicking "request takedown," you will be taken to a form to provide further details about the request.
- **Via Email Communication**: For social media threat exposures, communicate with a Flare Customer Success Manager. Flare will provide a form for you to fill in and start the takedown process.

### Confirmation and Tracking

- Once the forms are completed, you will receive an email and/or in-app confirmation, including a ticket tracking link and status updates.
- Flare conducts the takedown request process, which varies by use case. If additional information is required, you will be notified via email.
- After processing, a takedown confirmation is provided via email and/or within the app, indicating whether the request was successful or unsuccessful.

## Important Notes

- Submission does not guarantee success, as outcomes depend on the request's nature and third-party cooperation.
- Generally, the more obviously malicious the threat exposure or the more clearly it violates third-party terms of service, the higher the likelihood of a successful takedown.

For more details, visit the official documentation: [Flare.io takedown-services](https://docs.flare.io/takedown-services).
