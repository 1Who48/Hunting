## Illicit Networks

Handling Illicit Networks in Flare.io

## Overview

Illicit networks, often referred to as the dark web or darknet, are hidden parts of the internet where cybercriminals engage in various illicit activities. These networks encompass specialized websites focusing on areas such as drugs, fraud, hacking, or specific geographical regions, while others offer a broader range of illegal goods and services. Each platform typically has its own set of rules outlining permissible and prohibited activities.

## Key Components of Illicit Networks

### 1. Markets

Underground markets, also known as crypto-markets, are platforms where individuals list and sell illegal products and services, including drugs, fraudulent documents, and hacking tools. Transactions on these markets are primarily conducted using cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin and Monero.  

### 2. Account Shops

These are specialized markets where cybercriminals sell access to compromised accounts from various services and websites.  

### 3. Infected Devices

Referred to as "bots," these listings involve credentials and cookies obtained from personal computers infected by malware. Such listings typically include the date of data theft, partial IP addresses, and the victim's country of origin. Notably, breaches like the 2021 Electronic Arts incident have been linked to such compromised devices.  

### 4. Forum Posts

Forums within illicit networks serve as anonymous spaces where actors discuss and collaborate on topics like hacking, fraud, politics, and more.  

### 5. Profiles

These are pages representing individual actors on markets and forums, providing insights into their activities and reputations within the community.  

### 6. Ransom Leaks

Since 2019, ransomware groups have publicly listed their victims on dedicated websites, often sharing proof of data breaches, payment deadlines, and cryptocurrency ransom addresses. Some platforms even host auctions for the leaked data.  

Illicit blogs function similarly to legitimate blogs but focus on content related to the cybercrime ecosystem and cyber threat intelligence news.

## Responding to Illicit Network Events

When alerted to activities on illicit networks, it's crucial to assess the risk and determine appropriate actions:

### - Market Listings

Evaluate the content to understand the threat level. If an employee account is compromised, prompt password changes and additional security measures are advisable.  

### - Forum Posts and Chat Messages

Analyze the context to gauge potential risks. Understanding the credibility of the actor and the nature of the discussion can inform response strategies.  

### - Ransom Leaks

If your organization is directly affected, immediate incident response actions are necessary. For third-party breaches, assess potential impacts on your operations and engage with the affected party as needed.  

### - Infected Devices

These alerts are time-sensitive. Identify the affected user or system, reset credentials, and monitor for further suspicious activities to prevent unauthorized access.  

Understanding and monitoring illicit networks are vital for organizations aiming to protect their assets, reputation, and stakeholders from cyber threats originating from these hidden parts of the internet.
