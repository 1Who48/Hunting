## Supply Chain Ransomware Exposure Monitoring

## Overview

Flare's **Supply Chain Ransomware Exposure Monitoring** provides users with precise and timely information about the level of exposure their business has from ransomware attacks affecting their third parties and extended supply chain. This helps businesses identify and respond to these specific threat exposures within minutes of their appearance.

## Key Features

1. **Visibility Across Known and Unknown Supply Chain Entities**: Leverages Flare's entire database of threat intelligence combined with information specific to your Tenant to extend visibility into threats affecting third parties and even fourth parties.
2. **Rapid and Precise Risk Determination**: AI-powered file path analysis quantifies risk associated with threat exposures found in supply chain ransomware incidents.
3. **Near Real-Time Monitoring and Alerting**: Dynamic and world-class collection of the most active ransomware groups and their platforms for disclosing attacks, updated in near real time.
4. **Boost GRC Efforts**: Provides tangible and contextual intelligence valuable in a changing regulatory environment with higher standards around transparency in disclosing breaches.

## How It Works

### Setting Up Identifiers

- Supply Chain Monitoring will begin compiling relevant results and information automatically based on the Identifiers in your Tenant.
- To review what's been identified in your Tenant so far, visit the Supply Chain Monitoring page.
- The supply chain analysis is only run for identifiers that have the "Ransom Leaks" category enabled. Results will predominantly be found using Keyword identifiers. Therefore, to see results in the Supply Chain Monitoring page, make sure you have set up Keyword identifiers with the "Ransom Leak" category activated.

### Ignoring and Remediating Events

- If you ignore an event in the main event feed, it will also be ignored in the Supply Chain table.

For more detailed information, you can visit the Flare Supply Chain Ransomware Exposure Monitoring documentation.
